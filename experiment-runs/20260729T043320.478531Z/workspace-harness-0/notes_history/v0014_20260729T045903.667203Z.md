# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Yellow (4) is wall/background; green (3) and gray (5) form corridors/chambers.
- Avatar is a 5x5 block: two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows; current origin is x19,y30.
- Blue/black glyph near x16..18,y31..33 is a directional floor switch.
- Upper red/black glyph is a locked goal/door.
- Bottom-left 10x6 panel records directional switch state; cyan (11) in rows 61..62 is the remaining input budget.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- Recorded mapping: ACTION1 up, ACTION2 down, ACTION3 left, ACTION4 right, in 5-cell increments.
- Switch contact is directional: upward contact changes the middle HUD rows and is free; horizontal contact changes the outer HUD rows.
- The latest ACTION4 crossing from the switch's left side changed the outer HUD rows despite prior upward activation and consumed one meter tick.
- A prior ACTION3 contact changed the same outer rows when the switch was not yet activated. Thus the reusable rule currently supported is horizontal-versus-vertical HUD encoding, though side-specific effects remain possible.
- HUD likely records a directional sequence at the switch; satisfying it may unlock the upper door.
- Ordinary inputs spend one cyan column, including ordinary wall collisions. Some switch/checkpoint contacts and the upper-lock interaction are free.
- An input after meter exhaustion is swallowed and resets in-level to the lower checkpoint without GAME_OVER.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Immediate adjacency to the upper glyph completes the level: false.
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: false.
- All blocked ACTION1 inputs are free: false.
- Switch only responds to right-to-left ACTION3: false; ACTION1 and ACTION4 contacts also produce effects.
- Switch contact has one direction-independent effect: false; upward and horizontal approaches affect different HUD segments.
- Horizontal HUD update is only allowed before a shared activation flag: falsified by transition 58; ACTION4 updated outer rows after ACTION1 activation.
- Replacing ACTION3 behavior entirely with ACTION4 behavior was falsified by full-history backtest at transition 24; both observed horizontal directions must be represented.
- Meter exhaustion immediately emits GAME_OVER: false.
