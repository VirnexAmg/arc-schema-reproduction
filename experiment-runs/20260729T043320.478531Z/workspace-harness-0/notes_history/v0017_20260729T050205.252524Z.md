## Mechanism hypotheses
- ACTION1/2/3/4 map to up/down/left/right in 5-cell increments.
- The switch is a directional FSM. Upward contact and corridor entry affect the middle HUD pair; horizontal contact affects outer rows.
- ACTION4 contacts alternate the middle pair by latent parity: odd selects x7..8, even selects x3..4.
- Outer HUD rows latch on initial horizontal activation. A later corridor reset can restore their visible base pattern without clearing latent horizontal phase; later ACTION4 contacts then alter only the middle pair.
- The third ACTION4 contact is a free validated FSM transition, unlike the first two paid contacts. This phase-dependent cost rule fits all history but needs a broader explanation.
- Downward entry from y25 to y30 is stateful: first entry selects right middle pair and waives cost; repeated/post-timeout entry resets the full glyph and consumes cost.
- Timeout restores avatar and visible HUD but preserves corridor-entry/contact phase.
- HUD likely encodes a directional sequence that unlocks the upper door.
- Ordinary inputs spend one cyan column, including ordinary wall collisions; recognized FSM/checkpoint transitions and upper-lock interaction can be free.

## Falsified / ruled out

- Switch responds only to ACTION3 or has one direction-independent effect: false.
- ACTION3 and ACTION4 are identical: false.
- Repeated ACTION4 leaves the middle segment unchanged: false.
- One-way ACTION4 threshold with all contacts after second selecting left: false; third selected right.
- Every ACTION4 contact rewrites outer rows: false. On the latest contact, outer rows retained their reset pattern while only the middle pair changed.
- Every ordinary third ACTION4 contact consumes meter: false; latest contact was free.
- Meter exhaustion immediately emits GAME_OVER: false.
- Every ACTION2 y30 entry is free/right-selecting: false.
- Timeout clears all FSM state: false.
