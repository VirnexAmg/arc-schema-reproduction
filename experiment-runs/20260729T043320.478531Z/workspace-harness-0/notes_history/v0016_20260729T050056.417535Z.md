- Avatar is a 5x5 block with two color-12 rows over three color-9 rows; current origin is x19,y30.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- Recorded mapping: ACTION1 up, ACTION2 down, ACTION3 left, ACTION4 right, in 5-cell increments.
- Switch contact is directional and stateful: upward contact changes the middle HUD pair; horizontal contact changes the outer HUD rows.
- Repeated ACTION4 contact advances horizontal-contact state: the second observed rightward approach returned the middle HUD pair from x7..8 to x3..4 while retaining the horizontal outer pattern.
- Downward entry from y25 to y30 is also stateful: the first recorded entry selected the right middle pair and waived cost; a repeated/post-timeout entry resets the full six-row glyph and consumes cost.
- Timeout restores avatar/HUD but preserves the latent corridor-entry phase. This persistence is required to explain the latest post-timeout ACTION2 transition.
- HUD likely encodes a directional sequence at the switch; satisfying the required sequence may unlock the upper door.
- Ordinary inputs spend one cyan column, including ordinary wall collisions. Some first-contact/checkpoint interactions and the upper-lock interaction are free.
- An input after meter exhaustion is swallowed and resets in-level without GAME_OVER.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Immediate adjacency to the upper glyph completes the level: false.
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: false.
- All blocked ACTION1 inputs are free: false.
- Switch only responds to ACTION3: false; ACTION1 and ACTION4 contacts also change HUD state.
- Switch contact has one direction-independent effect: false.
- Horizontal HUD update is only allowed before a shared activation flag: falsified by transition 58.
- Repeated ACTION4 leaves the middle HUD segment unchanged: falsified by transition 60.
- Treating ACTION3 and ACTION4 identically throughout was falsified by full-history replay.
- Meter exhaustion immediately emits GAME_OVER: false.
- Latest falsification: every ACTION2 entry at y30 is a free transition selecting the right middle pair. The repeated post-timeout entry instead consumed a tick and reset the full HUD to its base pattern.
- Latest falsification: timeout clears all directional FSM state. It clears the visible HUD but must preserve corridor-entry phase.
