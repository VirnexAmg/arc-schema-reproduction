# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Yellow (4) is wall/background; green (3) and gray (5) form corridors/chambers.
- Avatar is a 5x5 block: two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows. Current location is x19..23, y30..34.
- Upper gray chamber contains a separate red lock/goal glyph.
- Blue/black glyph at x16..18,y31..33 is a floor symbol or trigger that remains visible after the avatar moves adjacent/across it.
- Rows 61..62 contain an action-cost meter; each ordinary move changes the first 11 column to green.
- Bottom-left gray HUD glyph changed when the avatar entered the lower corridor at y30.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- Actions map from transitions as: 1 up, 2 down, 3 left, 4 right; each attempts a 5-cell displacement.
- Green and gray are traversable; yellow walls block movement.
- Entering the lower corridor triggers the bottom-left HUD change and waives that move's meter cost.
- H_symbol-passable: blue/black cells are traversable persistent substrate and are restored when uncovered.
- H_symbol-trigger: crossing or fully overlapping the blue/black glyph, rather than merely touching its edge, may trigger another state change.
- The upper red glyph may become traversable or complete the level only after satisfying lower-corridor objectives.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Attempting to overlap the upper red glyph immediately completes the level: falsified by blocked ACTION1.
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: falsified by blocked ACTION2, which advanced it.
- Every blocked action advances meter: falsified by blocked ACTION1.
- Touching any blue/black pixel collects and clears the glyph: falsified by ACTION4 from x14..18 to x19..23; the glyph remained unchanged.
- The y30 corridor-entry move consumes a normal meter tick: falsified; its HUD/avatar changes occurred without advancing the strip.
