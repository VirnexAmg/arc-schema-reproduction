# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Yellow (4) is wall/background; green (3) and gray (5) form corridors/chambers.
- Avatar is a 5x5 block with two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows; currently at x29..33,y25..29.
- Blue/black glyph at x16..18,y31..33 is a persistent directional floor switch.
- Upper red/black glyph is a locked goal/door. Bottom-left gray panel is a state/instruction HUD; rows 61..62 are a finite action-cost meter, now showing one color-11 column remaining.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- Certified action mapping: 1 up, 2 down, 3 left, 4 right, in 5-cell increments.
- Green, gray, blue, and black are traversable; yellow and the unsatisfied upper glyph block.
- Entering the lower corridor changes one HUD feature and waives that action's meter tick.
- Approaching the floor switch from its right with ACTION3 activated/rotated the HUD glyph while the switch persisted.
- HUD may encode a multi-approach directional sequence to perform at the floor switch; completing it should unlock the upper door.
- Most post-activation actions consume meter, while an upward interaction against the locked door at y15 is free.
- Meter exhaustion may either trigger GAME_OVER immediately or permit a zero-meter state until another costly input.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Immediate adjacency with the upper red glyph completes the level: false.
- Every blocked input after activation consumes meter: false at the upper lock.
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: false; blocked ACTION2 advanced it.
- Touching switch edge collects it: false; it persists.
- Simple edge adjacency or arbitrary overlap activates it: false; direction of approach matters.

## Needs testing
- Immediate consequence of consuming the final meter column.
- Whether another right-to-left switch approach advances the HUD sequence or repeated approaches are ignored.
- Required approach directions/order encoded by HUD and exact LEVEL_COMPLETE condition.
