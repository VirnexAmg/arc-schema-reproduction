# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Yellow (4) is wall/background; green (3) and gray (5) form corridors/chambers.
- Avatar is a 5x5 block with two color-12 rows over three color-9 rows; currently pressed against the upper lock at origin x34,y15.
- Blue/black glyph at x16..18,y31..33 is a directional floor switch.
- Upper red/black glyph is a locked goal/door.
- Bottom-left 10x6 panel records directional switch state; cyan (11) in rows 61..62 is the remaining input budget.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- ACTION1/2/3/4 map to up/down/left/right in 5-cell increments.
- Directional contacts with the central glyph update the bottom-left HUD as an FSM.
- Three rightward switch contacts complete and latch a horizontal sub-sequence; the third is free, later right contacts retain the display but cost meter.
- A repeated downward corridor entry resets the six-row glyph only before the three-contact horizontal sub-sequence is complete. Once right_contacts >= 3, later vertical traversal preserves the latched HUD.
- Timeout restores avatar and visible HUD but preserves corridor-entry/contact phase.
- The HUD likely encodes a longer directional sequence that unlocks the upper door; completing only the horizontal sub-sequence is insufficient.
- Ordinary inputs spend one cyan column, including ordinary wall collisions; recognized FSM/checkpoint transitions and upper-lock interaction can be free.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Immediate adjacency to upper glyph completes the level: false.
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: false.
- Switch responds only to ACTION3 or has one direction-independent effect: false.
- ACTION3 and ACTION4 are identical: false.
- Repeated ACTION4 leaves the middle segment unchanged: false.
- All contacts from the third onward are free: false; fourth right contact consumed a meter tick.
- Unbounded odd/even alternation of right contacts: false; fourth contact retained the right middle pair rather than selecting left.
- Every ACTION4 contact rewrites outer rows: false.
- Meter exhaustion immediately emits GAME_OVER: false.
- Every repeated ACTION2 entry at y30 resets the HUD: false; after the completed three-right-contact sequence it remained latched.
- Timeout clears all FSM state: false.
- Three rightward switch contacts alone unlock the upper door: false. The latest ACTION1 at x34,y15 was blocked, frame unchanged, and emitted no LEVEL_COMPLETE.
