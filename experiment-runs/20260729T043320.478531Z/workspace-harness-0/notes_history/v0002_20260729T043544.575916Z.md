# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- 64x64 board: yellow (4) background/walls and green (3) traversable corridors.
- Movable 5x5 avatar: top two rows color 12, bottom three rows color 9. Current footprint x30..34, y15..19.
- Upper gray (5) chamber contains a separate dark-red (9) lock/goal glyph.
- Small blue/black glyph (colors 0/1) near x16,y31 is likely a key or required collectible.
- Bottom rows 61..62 are a movement meter: one color-11 column becomes green per successful move; blocked inputs do not advance it.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- H_move: ACTION1/2/3/4 move up/down/left/right by 5 pixels. A move succeeds only when newly occupied cells are traversable colors 3 or 5; red goal pixels are currently collision-solid.
- H_key: the blue/black glyph must be collected before the upper lock can be entered or activated. Likely route from the current blocked door position is down to the broad corridor, left to the glyph, then return.
- H_switch alternative: the blue/black glyph is a switch that changes the door remotely rather than a carried key.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Falsified: merely overlapping/reaching the red upper glyph emits LEVEL_COMPLETE. At x30,y15, ACTION1 attempted to enter it, but the entire frame remained unchanged and no level completed.
- Falsified: every input advances the bottom strip. The blocked ACTION1 did not advance it.
- Direct upward passage is blocked by red pixels at the chamber entrance.
