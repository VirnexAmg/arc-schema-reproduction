## Mechanism hypotheses
- Actions 1/2/3/4 move up/down/left/right by 5 cells when the destination is traversable.
- Successful movement consumes one meter tick.
- H_cost: blocked ACTION2 also consumes a tick, unlike the observed blocked ACTION1. Meter cost may be action-specific or interaction-dependent.
- H_key: touching the blue/black glyph collects a key that permits entry to the upper lock.
- H_switch: touching the glyph remotely changes/opens the lock instead.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Merely attempting to overlap the upper red glyph completes the level: falsified by blocked ACTION1 from the doorway.
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: falsified by blocked ACTION2 from x29..33,y25..29, which advanced only the meter.
- Every blocked action advances the meter: falsified by the blocked ACTION1 door attempt.
