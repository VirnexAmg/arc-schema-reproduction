## Mechanism hypotheses
- Recorded action mapping: ACTION1 up, ACTION2 down, ACTION3 left, ACTION4 right, in 5-cell increments.
- Green/gray and blue/black switch cells are traversable; yellow and unsatisfied upper lock are solid.
- The switch responds to a particular approach direction, with right-to-left (ACTION3) already implicated.
- HUD likely requests or records a directional sequence at the switch; solving it unlocks the upper door.
- Most post-activation actions consume one cyan meter column; special interactions may be free.
- When the cyan meter is already empty, the next input is swallowed and causes an in-level reset: avatar respawns directly on the lower 5x5 red checkpoint origin, checkpoint footprint is cleared, HUD and meter reset. No GAME_OVER event occurs.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Immediate adjacency to the upper red glyph completes the level: false.
- Every blocked post-activation action consumes meter: false for upward interaction at upper lock.
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: false; blocked ACTION2 advanced it.
- Touching the switch from any direction necessarily activates it: false; approach direction matters.
- Meter exhaustion immediately emits GAME_OVER: false.
- Timeout ACTION4 performing an ordinary move, reset-then-move, or respawning left of the checkpoint: false. Backtest now confirms the input is swallowed and respawn is on the checkpoint origin.
