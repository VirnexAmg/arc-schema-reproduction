# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Yellow (4) is wall/background; green (3) and gray (5) form corridors/chambers.
- Avatar is a 5x5 block with two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows; current frame places it at x29..33,y45..49 after a puzzle checkpoint reset.
- Blue/black glyph at x16..18,y31..33 is a directional floor switch.
- Upper red/black glyph is a locked goal/door.
- Bottom-left gray panel is a directional/status HUD. Rows 61..62 contain a 42-column cyan (11) action budget plus fixed icons.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- Action mapping from transitions: 1 up, 2 down, 3 left, 4 right, generally in 5-cell increments.
- Green, gray, blue, and black are traversable; yellow and the unsatisfied upper glyph block.
- Approaching the floor switch from its right with ACTION3 changes the HUD glyph.
- The HUD likely encodes a directional sequence to perform at the switch; completion should unlock the upper door.
- Most actions after activation consume one cyan budget column, while some interaction/entry events are free.
- Newly confirmed: when the final budget column has already been consumed, the next action causes an in-level checkpoint reset: avatar reappears at the lower red checkpoint (x29,y45), budget refills to 42 cyan columns, and HUD resets. This is NOT GAME_OVER.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Immediate adjacency with upper red glyph completes the level: false.
- Every blocked input after activation consumes meter: false at upper lock.
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: false; blocked ACTION2 advanced it.
- Touching switch edge from any direction collects it: false; it persists and approach direction matters.
- Meter exhaustion immediately emits GAME_OVER: false. It produces an in-level reset on the following input.
- Prior model prediction that exhausted-meter ACTION4 was an ordinary right move is falsified.
