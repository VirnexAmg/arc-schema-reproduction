## Mechanism hypotheses
- ACTION1/2/3/4 map to up/down/left/right in 5-cell increments.
- Directional contacts with the central glyph update the bottom-left HUD as an FSM.
- Both horizontal directions trigger switch contact. The latest ACTION3 immediately after ACTION4 restored the outer-row pattern, disproving the prior symbol_activated gate.

- A repeated downward corridor entry resets the glyph only before the three-contact horizontal sub-sequence is complete; once right_contacts >= 3, vertical traversal preserves it.
- Timeout restores avatar and visible HUD but preserves corridor-entry/contact phase.
- The HUD likely encodes a longer directional sequence that unlocks the upper door; completing only the horizontal sub-sequence is insufficient.
- Ordinary inputs spend one cyan column, including ordinary wall collisions; recognized FSM/checkpoint transitions and upper-lock interaction can be free.
