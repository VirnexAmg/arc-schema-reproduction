# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- 64x64 board with yellow (4) background and green (3) traversable maze/corridor.
- Movable 5x5 token: top two rows color 12, bottom three rows color 9. Current footprint is x30..34, y45..49.
- Upper gray (5) chamber contains a dark-red (9) goal glyph.
- Bottom rows 61..62 contain a progress/time strip: every recorded action converts exactly one color-11 cell to color 3 from left to right.
- Small blue/black mark near x16,y31 appears static and may be a collectible or decoration.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- H_move: actions are grid moves inferred from transitions: ACTION1 moves token up 5 cells, ACTION2 down 5, ACTION3 left 5, ACTION4 right 5. Destination must be traversable rather than yellow background.
- H_goal: token must route around the yellow notch, enter the narrow upper corridor, and reach the gray/red chamber.
- Alternative H_timer: bottom strip may be a strict action timer rather than progress; reaching the chamber before it expires is required.

## Evidence / ruled out
- Sequence 1,2,3,4 repeated shows up/down/left/right movement and restoration of vacated footprint to green.
- Direct repeated upward movement from the current position should eventually be blocked by the yellow notch at x29..33, y30..39; therefore a left detour is needed.
