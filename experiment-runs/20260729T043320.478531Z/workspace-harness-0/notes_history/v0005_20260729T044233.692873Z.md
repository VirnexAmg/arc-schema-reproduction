# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Yellow (4) is wall/background; green (3) and gray (5) form corridors/chambers.
- Avatar is a 5x5 block with two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows.
- The blue/black glyph at x16..18,y31..33 is persistent floor substrate restored after the avatar leaves.
- Upper red glyph is a locked goal/door. Bottom-left gray panel is a state HUD; rows 61..62 are an action-cost meter.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- Recorded action mapping: 1 up, 2 down, 3 left, 4 right, in 5-cell increments.
- Green, gray, blue, and black are traversable; yellow and the unsatisfied upper red door block.
- Entering the lower corridor changes one HUD feature and waives that action's meter tick.
- H_cross: fully overlapping/crossing the blue/black floor glyph changes the bottom-left HUD by filling x5..6 on rows 55,56,59,60 red.
- H_depart: the HUD change occurs on departing the glyph rather than entering it.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Immediate overlap with the upper red glyph completes the level: false (blocked ACTION1).
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: false (a blocked ACTION2 advanced it).
- Every blocked action advances meter: false (blocked ACTION1 did not).
- Touching the blue/black glyph edge collects/clears it: false; it persists.
- Earlier implementation's symbol trigger detection is falsified by backtest mismatch at transition 23; despite ACTION3 producing the HUD change, the model emitted no HUD delta. The issue may be trigger timing or latent detection, not passability.

- Exact trigger phase: entry, full overlap, or departure.
- Conditions opening the upper lock and producing LEVEL_COMPLETE.
