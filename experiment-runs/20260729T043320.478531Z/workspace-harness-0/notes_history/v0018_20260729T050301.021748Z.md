# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Yellow (4) is wall/background; green (3) and gray (5) form corridors/chambers.
- Avatar is a 5x5 block with two color-12 rows over three color-9 rows; current origin is x19,y30.
- Blue/black glyph at x16..18,y31..33 is a directional floor switch.
- Upper red/black glyph is a locked goal/door.
- Bottom-left 10x6 panel records directional switch state; cyan (11) in rows 61..62 is the remaining input budget.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- ACTION1/2/3/4 map to up/down/left/right in 5-cell increments.
- Directional contacts with the central glyph update the bottom-left HUD as an FSM.
- Right contacts 1 and 2 choose the right and left middle pair respectively; contact 3 completes a sub-sequence, is free, and latches the right pair. Later right contacts retain the right display but resume ordinary meter cost.
- Outer HUD rows latch on initial horizontal activation. A corridor reset restores their visible base pattern without clearing latent contact phase.
- First downward corridor entry selects the right middle pair and is free; repeated/post-timeout entry resets the full glyph and consumes cost.
- Timeout restores avatar and visible HUD but preserves corridor-entry/contact phase.
- HUD likely encodes a directional sequence that unlocks the upper door.
- Ordinary inputs spend one cyan column, including ordinary wall collisions; recognized FSM/checkpoint transitions and upper-lock interaction can be free.
