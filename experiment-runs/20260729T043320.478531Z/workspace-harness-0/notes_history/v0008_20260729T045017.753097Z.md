# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Yellow (4) is wall/background; green (3) and gray (5) form corridors/chambers.
- Avatar is a 5x5 block with two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows.
- The blue/black glyph at x16..18,y31..33 is a directional floor switch restored after the avatar leaves.
- Upper red/black glyph is a locked goal/door. Bottom-left gray panel is a state HUD; rows 61..62 are an action-cost meter.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- Certified action mapping: 1 up, 2 down, 3 left, 4 right, in 5-cell increments.
- Green, gray, blue, and black are traversable; yellow and the unsatisfied upper glyph block.
- Entering the lower corridor changes one HUD feature and waives that action's meter tick.
- The floor switch activates when approached from the right using ACTION3. Activation rotates/changes the matching HUD glyph while the floor glyph persists.
- After switch activation, ordinary inputs consume meter ticks, including many blocked inputs. A blocked ACTION1 while the avatar is already seated at y15 against the upper lock is specifically a free interaction attempt.
- Likely objective: perform a sequence of directional approaches to the persistent switch encoded by the HUD, then return to the upper lock.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Immediate overlap/adjacency with the upper red glyph completes the level: false; repeated ACTION1 at y15 is blocked and the latest attempt leaves the entire frame unchanged.
- Previous model rule that every blocked input after switch activation consumes a meter tick: false. The y15 upward lock interaction is free; revised model now backtests all 40 transitions.
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: false (a blocked ACTION2 advanced it).
- Every blocked action universally advances meter: false both before activation and at the upper lock.
- Touching the blue/black glyph edge collects/clears it: false; it persists.
- Simple edge adjacency or any overlap activates the switch: false; vertical passage near/over it did not produce the final HUD rotation.

## Needs testing
- Whether the HUD specifies multiple directional switch approaches and which approach comes next.
- Whether completing that sequence unlocks the upper glyph.
- Exact LEVEL_COMPLETE condition.
- Whether meter exhaustion causes GAME_OVER and which interaction events waive costs.
