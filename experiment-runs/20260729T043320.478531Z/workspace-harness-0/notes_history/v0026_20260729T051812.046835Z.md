# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Yellow (4) is wall/background; green (3) and gray (5) form traversable corridors/chambers.
- Avatar is a 5x5 block with two color-12 rows over three color-9 rows.
- The small blue/black glyph in the maze is a directional switch; the upper red/black glyph is a locked goal/door.
- The bottom-left 10x6 panel is the switch/FSM display. The cyan strip in rows 61..62 is the remaining ordinary-input budget.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- Recorded controls: ACTION1 up, ACTION2 down, ACTION3 left, ACTION4 right, in 5-cell increments.
- Switch contacts update a directional finite-state display rather than simply collecting the glyph.
- Three right contacts form a recognized horizontal subsequence; the third is free. A later left-contact reversal opens another phase.
- Repeated late left contacts restore the canonical outer glyph and toggle the middle pair. After the second such contact, an immediate up/down reversal preserves middle-right but rotates the outer rows to split-top/solid-bottom.
- This current rotated projection may mark completion of a vertical subphase, potentially enabling another switch contact or the upper lock.
- Timeout restores avatar/HUD/budget but preserves some latent switch phase.
- Ordinary successful moves and ordinary collisions spend one cyan column. Recognized switch/checkpoint transitions and upper-lock attempts can be free.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Immediate adjacency to the upper glyph completes the level: false.
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: false.
- ACTION3 and ACTION4 are identical: false.
- Three right contacts alone unlock the door: false.
- Timeout clears all FSM state: false.
- Horizontal contacts always overwrite all six HUD rows: false.
- Every late left contact has the same canonical result: false.
- Every canonical restoration is free: false.
- The latest late-phase ACTION2 preserves the entire canonical glyph: false. It rotates the outer rows while retaining middle-right.
- The latest late-phase ACTION2 selects middle-left merely because it immediately follows ACTION1: false; that only occurred before the second canonical left interrogation.
