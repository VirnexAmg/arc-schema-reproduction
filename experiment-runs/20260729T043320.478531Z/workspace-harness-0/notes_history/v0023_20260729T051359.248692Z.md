# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Yellow (4) is wall/background; green (3) and gray (5) form corridors/chambers.
- Avatar is a 5x5 block with two color-12 rows over three color-9 rows; currently beside the central blue/black directional switch.
- Upper red/black glyph is a locked goal/door.
- Bottom-left 10x6 panel records directional switch/FSM state; cyan (11) in rows 61..62 is the remaining input budget.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- ACTION1/2/3/4 map to up/down/left/right in 5-cell increments.
- Directional contact with the central glyph updates the HUD as an FSM: horizontal approaches latch outer rows and alter the middle pair; vertical corridor entry selects the middle pair.
- Three rightward contacts complete a horizontal sub-sequence; the third contact is free, while later contacts cost meter.
- After this horizontal phase is latched, repeated downward entry records the left middle pair; upward approach records the right pair. This is direction-state, not a cyan-meter threshold.
- Timeout restores avatar and visible HUD but preserves corridor-entry/contact phase.
- Ordinary inputs spend one cyan column, including ordinary wall collisions; recognized FSM/checkpoint transitions and upper-lock interaction can be free.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Immediate adjacency to upper glyph completes the level: false.
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: false.
- ACTION3 and ACTION4 are identical: false.
- Three rightward switch contacts alone unlock the upper door: false.
- Timeout clears all FSM state: false.
- ACTION3 horizontal contact is disabled once symbol_activated is true: false.
- After right_contacts >= 3, downward corridor re-entry leaves the entire HUD unchanged: false.
- Latest mismatch falsified the over-specific theory that the downward left-pair update requires a late meter threshold; it is better explained as a reusable post-latch vertical-direction transition.
