# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Yellow (4) is wall/background; green (3) and gray (5) form corridors/chambers.
- Avatar is a 5x5 block with two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows.
- The blue/black glyph at x16..18,y31..33 is a directional floor switch restored after the avatar leaves.
- Upper red glyph is a locked goal/door. Bottom-left gray panel is a state HUD; rows 61..62 are an action-cost meter.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- Certified action mapping: 1 up, 2 down, 3 left, 4 right, in 5-cell increments.
- Green, gray, blue, and black are traversable; yellow and the unsatisfied upper red door block.
- Entering the lower corridor changes one HUD feature and waives that action's meter tick.
- The blue/black switch activates only when approached from the right using ACTION3. Activation rotates/changes the matching HUD glyph while the floor glyph persists.
- Meter rules are state-dependent: after directional-switch activation, every input (including a blocked ACTION1 at the door) consumes one tick; interaction steps can waive a tick. Before activation, at least some blocked ACTION1 inputs are free while ACTION2 consumes a tick.
- Likely objective: activate directional switch(es), then return through the upper stem to unlock the red door, possibly after satisfying further directional/HUD conditions.

## Falsified / ruled out
- Immediate overlap with the upper red glyph completes the level: false (blocked ACTION1).
- Meter advances iff movement succeeds: false (blocked ACTION2 advanced it).
- Every blocked action universally advances meter: false before switch activation (blocked ACTION1 did not).
- Meter behavior is independent of switch state: false; the latest blocked ACTION1 after activation advanced x38 on rows 61..62.
- Touching the blue/black glyph edge collects/clears it: false; it persists.
- Simple edge adjacency trigger was false: vertical passage near/over the glyph did not trigger.
- Trigger on any overlap was false: ACTION2 into the lower corridor overlapped/passed the glyph without final HUD rotation.
