# Working notes

## Objects
- Movable 5x5 token: color 12 on its top two rows and color 9 on its bottom three.
- Color-3 corridors and color-5 room floor are traversable; color 4 is wall.
- A 0/1 directional control glyph lies in the left branch. It is traversable, hidden under the token, and restored when vacated.
- Upper color-9 motif in a color-5 chamber is the likely goal/lock.
- Bottom-left 9-pattern is a directional status indicator. Crossing the 0/1 glyph changes it based on approach direction.
- Two-row color-11 strip is movement budget/progress; ordinary successful moves consume one pixel.

## Established mechanisms
- Movement is exactly 5 pixels: ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left, ACTION4=right.
- Destination footprint may contain 0/1/3/5; 9/12/4 obstruct movement under the current model.
- Vacated cells restore underlying color-3 or color-5 floor; the control glyph is restored specially.
- Crossing the control downward changes the status pattern and refunds that move; crossing upward produces a different pattern and consumes the move.
- Full Timeline backtest passes 15 transitions.

## Goal hypotheses
- H1 (preferred): set the directional status by crossing the control upward, then return to the upper 9 motif and push/collide upward to unlock it.
- H2: the upper motif requires matching one of several directional status patterns, but the needed approach direction is not yet known.
- H3: the upper motif is merely another control and a separate completion condition follows.

## Experiments
- Direct upward motion into the upper motif before using the control was blocked.
- Entering the 0/1 glyph from above and then from below produced distinct bottom status patterns, establishing direction-sensitive interaction.
- Next: return to the upper chamber while retaining the current upward-crossing status, then test upward contact with the 9 motif.
