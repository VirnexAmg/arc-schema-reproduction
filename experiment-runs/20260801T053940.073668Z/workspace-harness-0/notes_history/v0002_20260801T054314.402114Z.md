# Working notes

## Grounded objects

- The compact color-0/1 motif at rows 32-34, columns 20-22 did not move under ACTION1. It is now better treated as a fixed interaction glyph than as the player.
- The movable object is a 5x5 tile: two color-12 rows over three color-9 rows. ACTION1 moved it exactly five cells upward, from rows 45-49 to rows 40-44, without changing its pattern.
- The large color-3 regions are traversable lanes for the 5x5 tile; color-4 gaps and other colored cells are candidate obstacles/interactions.
- On the same successful move, the leftmost cell of each color-11 status-bar row became color 3. The bar likely counts successful moves, time, or route length.

## Hypotheses

- H_marker_motion is rejected in its original form: the 0/1 motif is stationary under the first effective action.
- H_block_motion is supported: actions move the patterned 5x5 tile in tile-width translations when its destination is traversable color 3.
- H_panel_transform is revised and supported as H_panel_counter: the bottom bar advances by one cell after a successful tile move rather than being the primary manipulated object.
- H_cardinal_controls is active: other action ids likely map to other tile-width translations, but their vectors must be learned from transitions.

## Experiments

- Transition 1: ACTION1 moved the 12/9 tile upward by five and advanced the status bar; it left the 0/1 glyph and top glyph unchanged.
- Next probe ACTION2 from rows 40-44. A different translation supports H_cardinal_controls. A pattern-only change or no change would require a richer action model or could indicate a blocked direction.
