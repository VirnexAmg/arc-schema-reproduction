- The actionable object is a 5x5 token composed of two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows. It began at x=34..38, y=45..49.
- Color 3 is traversable floor for this token. Its vacated footprint is restored to color 3.
- The compact 0/1 glyph near x=20..22, y=31..33 stayed fixed under ACTION1 and is now more plausibly a destination or marker than the controlled object.
- The two color-11 rows in the lower strip form a consumable meter. ACTION1 replaced their leftmost column with color 3, reducing length 42 to 41.

## Stable hypotheses

- H_directional_control: ACTION1 translates the 5x5 token upward by one five-pixel tile when the destination footprint is entirely color 3. Other action-direction mappings remain ungrounded.
- H_panel_transform: the lower-strip change is best modeled as per-action resource consumption, not selection of the repeated 5/9 panels. Those panels may still encode goals.
- H_action_cost: every action consumes one column from the left edge of each color-11 meter row, including an action whose attempted move is blocked.
- H_marker_goal: the stationary 0/1 glyph is a likely target, checkpoint, or orientation cue for the movable token.
- H_identity_baseline is rejected for ACTION1 because the visible token and meter changed.
