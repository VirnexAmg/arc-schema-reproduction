# Working notes

## Grounded frame objects

- The 64x64 frame has a large upper arena and a separate-looking lower strip.
- The arena contains color-3 chambers, repeated 5/9 panels, a rare compact 0/1 glyph near the middle-left, and a 12/9 block near its bottom.
- The lower strip contains another 5/9 pattern, a long color-11 bar, and repeated color-8 marks. It may be status, inventory, or puzzle state.
- These are visual roles only; no causal role or action meaning has been observed.

## Stable hypotheses

- H_directional_control: actions locally move or reorient the rare 0/1 glyph while the chamber layout persists.
- H_panel_transform: actions transform or select a repeated panel, or update state represented by the lower strip; the 0/1 glyph may instead be a cursor.
- H_identity_baseline: before evidence, an action leaves the rendered frame unchanged. This is a falsifiable baseline, not a claimed mechanism.

## Experiments

- Probe ACTION1. Local glyph displacement or reorientation supports H_directional_control; a panel or lower-strip change supports H_panel_transform; no visible change provisionally supports H_identity_baseline or hidden state.
- Action meanings remain unassigned until transitions distinguish them. Compare changed cells and connected components after the probe.
