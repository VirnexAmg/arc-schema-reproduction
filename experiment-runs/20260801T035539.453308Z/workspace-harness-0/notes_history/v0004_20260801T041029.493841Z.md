# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- The controllable object is the 5x5 color-12/9 payload. Its two-row color-12 cap and three-row color-9 body remain unchanged during all six upward moves.
- The asymmetric blue/black glyph remains fixed in the left room and is a target, marker, or instruction candidate.
- The framed top color-9 glyph is an overlay-compatible panel, not a solid wall: the payload entered its lower portion on transition 6.
- The bottom color-11 strip is an action meter. Each action consumes its leftmost remaining cell on both rows.
- Motion is quantized into five-pixel cells. Color 4 is the strongest blocking-terrain candidate; observed destinations have used colors 3, 5, and 9.

## Stable hypotheses
- H_directional_move: ACTION1 moves the payload upward by one five-pixel cell and preserves its internal pattern.
- H_dock_target: the panel accepts the payload as an overlay. Moving once more should place it over the panel's central 5x5 pattern and may trigger a transform or boundary.
- H_solid_panel is rejected by transition 6.
- H_underlay_restore: a moving payload hides static terrain; vacated panel cells should restore the original color-5/3 structure.
- H_destructive_overlay: movement instead clears every vacated payload footprint to color 3, including panel cells.

## Evidence
- Transitions 1-5 moved the payload through color-3 cells from top row 45 to row 20.
- Transition 6 moved it from row 20 to row 15 despite mixed color-5/3 destination pixels. The payload overwrote the central five columns, its old shaft footprint became color 3, the meter advanced, and no level boundary occurred.
- This falsifies both the all-color-3 destination restriction and H_solid_panel.

## Next experiment
- Submit ACTION1 from top row 15. Both overlay hypotheses predict the payload moves to rows 10-14, centered on the panel pattern.
- H_underlay_restore predicts rows 15-19 return to their entry-frame colors: color 5 across the panel interior at row 15 and color 3 in the shaft below. H_destructive_overlay predicts those five central columns all become color 3.
- A level boundary or panel transformation would supersede both simple restoration predictions and must be encoded immediately.
