- H_directional_move: ACTION1 translates the payload five pixels upward when the entire destination cell is color 3. Five consecutive transitions matched this rule exactly.
- H_orientation_control is rejected: the payload retains its pattern and the blue/black glyph does not change.

- No level boundary occurred upon merely reaching the shaft end.
