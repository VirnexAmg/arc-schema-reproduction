## Stable hypotheses
- H_directional_move: ACTION1 moves up five pixels and ACTION2 moves down five pixels when the destination is traversable.
- H_action2_displace is supported by two consecutive downward moves.
- H_action3_left is supported: ACTION3 translates the payload left by five pixels when traversable.
- H_action3_right is rejected by the observed leftward translation.
- H_underlay_restore is supported: successful motion restores the exact static terrain beneath the vacated footprint.
- H_dock_target: top row 15 is an upper docking position that blocks ACTION1 but can be exited with ACTION2.
- ACTION4 remains untried. A rightward mapping is plausible by control symmetry, but a no-op or special interaction remains possible and is not encoded as fact.
