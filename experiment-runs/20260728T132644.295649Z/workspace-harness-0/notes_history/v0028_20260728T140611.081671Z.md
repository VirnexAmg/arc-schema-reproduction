# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Main playfield is a 5-pixel lattice maze (3=floor, 4=wall); token is a 5x5 object with two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows.
- Rows 61-62 contain the 42-step attempt strip and three status lamps.
- Level 1 had a reusable 0/1 glyph at lattice cell (19,30), a framed terminal at (34,15), a target glyph, and a 3x3 register on rows 55-60.
- The newly reached level 2 has a different maze. Its token starts at (34,40); visible embedded objects include a framed 9-pattern near (14,39), a 0/1 glyph near (49,46), another 11 glyph near (39,51), and an upper 11 glyph near (14,16).

## Established mechanisms
- ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left, ACTION4=right.
- Legal movement shifts one 5-pixel lattice cell; ordinary collisions generally consume an attempt.
- Attempt-strip exhaustion triggers checkpoint reset on the following action.
- On level 1, activating the input glyph transformed the register; returning to the framed terminal with register 111/001/101 and pressing ACTION1 submitted it and completed the level.

## Latest falsification / mismatch
- The previous model treated upward ACTION1 at the framed terminal as always inert and therefore predicted levels_completed=0. The real transition completed level 1 and replaced the entire board with level 2. Thus terminal ACTION1 is conditional submission, not an unconditional no-op; matching the displayed target is the acceptance condition supported by the successful trace.
- Rules and object coordinates may change after level-up, so the old level-1 route must not be trusted directly on level 2.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- H1: Every level presents one or more embedded operator glyphs; walking onto them transforms the lower 3x3 register, and a framed terminal submits when the register matches a displayed target.
- H2: The visible 0/1 and 11 glyphs may be multiple operators in level 2 rather than one operator plus one target.
- H3: Framing distinguishes terminal/target/operator roles, while raw bit patterns specify reusable register transforms.
