# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Main playfield is a 5-pixel lattice maze: color 3 is traversable corridor, color 4 is wall/exterior.
- Movable 5x5 token has color 12 in its top two rows and color 9 in its bottom three.
- Rows 61-62 hold a move/progress meter: legal ordinary moves convert the leftmost 11 column to 3.
- Embedded 0/1 glyph at the left branch is a reusable input cell.
- Framed color-5/9 pattern near x=32..40,y=8..16 appears to be a target glyph rather than mere decoration.
- Lower HUD rows 55-60 encode the current input-pattern phase.

## Established mechanisms
- ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left, ACTION4=right.
- Legal moves shift the token by one 5-pixel lattice cell. Destination cannot contain color 4 and must contain corridor 3 or an embedded 0/1 marker.
- Vacated input cell restores its 0/1 glyph.
- Ordinary legal moves advance the rows 61-62 meter.
- First observed input entry changes HUD rows 57-58 without advancing meter. Second entry changes rows 55-56 and 59-60 and does advance the meter. Full timeline backtest passes this phase model.

## Competing mechanism hypotheses
- H1: repeated glyph entry cycles the lower HUD until its pattern matches the framed upper target, then the level completes or an exit opens.
- H2: the glyph is one of multiple maze inputs, and exploration must locate further glyphs.
- H3: the upper framed glyph itself is an enterable goal reached through the vertical corridor.

## Falsified hypotheses
- Input glyph is a one-time collectible: ruled out because it reappears after leaving.
- Arbitrary color-5 regions are ordinary traversable floor: blocked upward evidence ruled this out when no corridor pixel was present.
