# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Main playfield is a 5-pixel lattice maze: color 3 is traversable corridor, color 4 is wall/exterior.
- Movable 5x5 token has color 12 in its top two rows and color 9 in its bottom three.
- Rows 61-62 hold a move/progress meter: each ordinary legal move converts the leftmost 11 column to 3.
- Embedded 0/1 glyphs in corridor cells are collectible markers and are enterable even though their footprint is not uniformly color 3.
- The framed color-5 pattern near x=32..40,y=8..16 is decorative/non-enterable, not a marker.
- Lower HUD rows 57-58 encode marker status in two-pixel segments.

## Established mechanisms
- ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left; ACTION4=right by remaining mapping (not directly tested).
- Legal moves shift the token by one 5-pixel lattice cell. A destination is legal if wall-free and either contains corridor color 3 or a 0/1 glyph.
- Vacated token pixels restore underlying corridor.
- Entering the 0/1 marker consumes it under the token and updates HUD rows 57-58: x=3..4 become color 5 while x=7..8 remain/become color 9. It does not advance the ordinary rows 61-62 meter.

## Mismatch resolution / falsified hypotheses
- The ACTION2 mismatch falsified treating every non-wall/no-corridor footprint alike and falsified the earlier assumption that marker entry merely lights x=7..8.
- Backtest showed decorative color-5 must remain blocked, while a footprint containing 0/1 is a special enterable marker.
- Exact marker HUD transition additionally clears the active x=3..4 segment to color 5 and preserves/lights x=7..8 as color 9.
- Full timeline now passes 13 transitions.

## Competing hypotheses
- The marker may be a required collectible rather than the final goal; likely additional glyphs or an endpoint must be visited.
- The upper framed pattern may be the eventual endpoint, despite being blocked until prerequisites are collected.
