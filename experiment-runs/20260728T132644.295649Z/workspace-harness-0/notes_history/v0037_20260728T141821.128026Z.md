# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Main playfield is a 5-pixel lattice maze (3=floor, 4=wall); token is a 5x5 object with two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows.
- Rows 61-62 contain the 42-column attempt strip and three status lamps.
- Level 1 had a reusable 0/1 glyph, framed terminal, target glyph, and 3x3 register.
- Level 2 contains a framed 9-pattern around x12..20,y38..46; a raw 0/1 glyph around x47..51,y46..48; and two raw 11 glyphs around x14..18,y16..18 and x39..43,y51..53.
- Current token occupies the consumed lower 11 cell at lattice position (39,50).

## Established mechanisms
- ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left, ACTION4=right.
- Legal movement shifts one 5-pixel lattice cell; ordinary collisions generally consume an attempt.
- Attempt-strip exhaustion triggers checkpoint reset on the following action.
- On level 1, activating the input glyph transformed the register; returning to the framed terminal with register 111/001/101 and pressing ACTION1 submitted it and completed the level.
- Level 2 spends two attempt-strip columns per ordinary action.
- Entering either raw 11 glyph refills all 42 attempt columns, spends no step, leaves the register unchanged, and consumes the glyph. The latest ACTION3 entered the lower glyph, made it vanish, and restored the full strip.
- The framed 9-pattern blocks direct downward traversal from (14,35); ACTION2 there was inert except for meter spending.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- H1: Raw 11 glyphs are reusable-instance checkpoint/refill operators; consuming the lower one may establish a new exhaustion respawn checkpoint as the upper one did.
- H2: The framed 9-pattern is the level-2 submission terminal or displayed target, not an enterable corridor cell.
- H3: The raw 0/1 glyph is the level-2 register-transform operator, analogous to level 1.
- H4: Framing distinguishes terminal/target roles while raw embedded glyphs identify consumable or transform operators.

## Ruled out
- Raw 11 glyphs are not decorative: both observed entries refilled the meter.
- Raw 11 glyphs are not reusable in place: each vanishes after entry/departure.
- The lower 11 is not behaviorally different from the upper refill operator on entry.
- Framed-terminal upward action is not always inert: matching the target completed level 1.
- Level coordinates/routes do not remain fixed after level-up.
- The framed 9-pattern is not directly passable from above.
