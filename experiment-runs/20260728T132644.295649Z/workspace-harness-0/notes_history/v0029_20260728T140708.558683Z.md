## Established mechanisms
- ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left, ACTION4=right.
- Legal movement shifts one 5-pixel lattice cell; ordinary collisions generally consume an attempt.
- Attempt-strip exhaustion triggers checkpoint reset on the following action.
- On level 1, activating the input glyph transformed the register; returning to the framed terminal with register 111/001/101 and pressing ACTION1 submitted it and completed the level.
- Level-2 first two moves confirm the same directional mapping and ordinary lattice movement: up then right.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- H1: Every level presents embedded operator glyphs; walking onto them transforms a hidden/displayed register, and a framed terminal submits when the register matches a target.
- H2: Level 2 has multiple operators (0/1 and 11 glyphs), with the framed 9-pattern likely terminal or target.
- H3: Framing distinguishes terminal/target/operator roles, while raw bit patterns specify reusable transforms.

## Ruled out
- Framed-terminal upward action is not always inert: matching the target completed level 1.
- Level coordinates/routes do not remain fixed after level-up.
