# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- Main playfield is a 5-pixel lattice maze: color 3 is traversable corridor, color 4 is exterior/wall.
- Movable 5x5 token is rendered with color 12 on its top two rows and color 9 on its bottom three rows.
- Successful moves advance a HUD meter on rows 61-62 by changing the leftmost color-11 column to color 3.
- A multicolor marker using colors 0/1 lies near lattice cell x=19..23, y=30..34 and is a likely destination.
- The framed pattern near x=32..40, y=8..16 appears to be an endpoint/display; color 5 can be underlying terrain near it.

## Established mechanisms
- ACTION1 moves the token upward by 5 pixels when legal.
- ACTION2 moves it downward by 5 pixels when legal.
- ACTION1 at token top-left (34,15) is blocked and causes an identity transition.
- Vacated token pixels restore hidden terrain inferred from adjacent cells in the same row.

## Competing hypotheses
- H1: ACTION3=left and ACTION4=right.
- H2: ACTION3=right and ACTION4=left.
- Legal destination likely requires some color-3 corridor and no color-4 wall; destination (34,10) is blocked because it contains only display colors 5/9.
- Goal may be moving the token onto the color-0/1 marker, but this remains untested.

## Experiments
- Repeated ACTION1 established upward movement and the top blockage.
- ACTION2 from the top endpoint established downward movement.

## Needs testing
- Discriminate horizontal action mapping with one ACTION3 or ACTION4 at the corridor junction.
- Verify collision rules and whether reaching the color-0/1 marker completes the level.
