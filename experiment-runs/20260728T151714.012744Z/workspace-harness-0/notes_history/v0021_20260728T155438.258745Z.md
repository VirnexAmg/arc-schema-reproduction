# Working notes

## Grounded objects and controls
- 64x64 maze built on 5x5 logical tiles.
- Recorded controls: ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left, ACTION4=right.
- Player is a 5x5 token (two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows).
- Color 3 is traversable route; colors 4/5 mostly walls/background. Embedded 0/1 and 11 glyphs are interactive mechanisms.
- Bottom color-11 strip is an action meter; level 2 spends two columns per ordinary input.

## Certified mechanisms
- Level 1 floor switch toggles status; upward activation at its upper dock completes level 1.
- Level 2 lower-right 0/1 switch is hazardous and changes warning/life status.
- Meter exhaustion has two outcomes selected by the warning glyph: when rows 57-58 have a right-hand mark at x=7..8, the next input produces GAME_OVER; the earlier left-hand warning phase instead recovers at checkpoint x=29,y=40, refills the meter, and restores the upper patterned glyph.
- The outer RESET after GAME_OVER independently restores the current level board. RESET transitions are not modeled by step().
- Entering the upper patterned tile at x=14,y=15 refills the meter; leaving it removes/reveals the glyph.
- Overall goal is WIN / 7 completed levels, not merely the first level-up.
- Full-history backtest passes 131/131 after distinguishing terminal versus recoverable exhaustion from the warning glyph.

## What the mismatch falsified
- It falsified the earlier unconditional claim that empty-meter input always respawns in-episode.
- It also falsified identifying terminal/recoverable exhaustion solely from the visible life marks or solely from upper-glyph restoration. The rows 57-58 warning side is the observed discriminator.
