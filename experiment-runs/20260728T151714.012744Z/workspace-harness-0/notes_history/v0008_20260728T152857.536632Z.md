# Working notes

## Objects
- 64x64 tile maze built from 5x5 logical cells.
- Player token is two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows.
- Color 3 is traversable floor; colors 4/5 are walls/background.
- Bottom color-11 strip is the ordinary-action budget meter.
- Embedded 0/1 glyphs are persistent floor switches and reappear after departure.
- Patterned chambers/status glyphs encode switch and terminal state.

## Established mechanisms
- ACTION1/2/3/4 are up/down/left/right by one 5-pixel tile.
- Traversable cells are solid color 3 or embedded 0/1 glyph cells; level 1's upper terminal has a special dock.
- Entering the level-1 glyph toggles the status display and does not consume ordinary meter; vacating it restores its pattern.
- In level 1 only, ACTION1 at the activated upper dock completes the level and loads level 2.
- Meter cost is level-dependent: level 1 ordinary actions consume one color-11 column; observed level-2 ordinary actions consume two.

## Latest mismatch revision
- Repeated ACTION1 from level-2 x=34,y=10 moved normally to y=5 and did not level up.
- This falsified treating the level-1 completion trigger as a coordinate/status shortcut reusable whenever x=34,y=15. Completion must additionally depend on level identity and likely on terminal-specific geometry/state. Model now restricts that known trigger to levels_completed==0.
