# Working notes

## Objects
- 64x64 tile maze built mostly from 5x5 logical cells.
- Player token is two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows.
- Color 3 is traversable floor; colors 4/5 are walls/background.
- Bottom color-11 strip is an ordinary-action budget meter.
- Embedded 0/1 glyphs are persistent trigger tiles and reappear after departure.
- Patterned chambers/status glyphs encode trigger and terminal state.

## Established controls and movement
- ACTION1/2/3/4 are up/down/left/right by one 5-pixel tile during ordinary movement.
- Traversable cells are solid color 3 or embedded 0/1 glyph cells; level 1's upper terminal has a special dock.
- Level-1 ordinary actions consume one meter column; observed level-2 ordinary actions consume two.
- In level 1, entering the glyph toggles the status display without ordinary meter cost, and ACTION1 at the activated upper dock completes the level.

## Latest mismatch revision
- In level 2 the player stood over the persistent glyph at x=49..53,y=45..49. ACTION1 did not make the expected ordinary move to x=49,y=40. Instead departure restored the glyph, teleported the player to x=30..34,y=40..44, refilled the color-11 meter, and changed the lower status/chamber patterns.
- This falsifies the hypothesis that level-2 glyphs behave merely as level-1-style status toggles followed by ordinary movement. The level-2 glyph is a teleporter/checkpoint or reset trigger whose effect occurs when leaving the covered trigger tile.

## Level 2 grounding
- Current player occupies x=30..34,y=40..44 after the glyph-triggered teleport.
- Persistent 0/1 glyph lies beneath/near the x=49..53,y=45..49 trigger tile.
- Patterned color-11 tile at x=39..43,y=50..54 may be a terminal.
- Patterned chamber near x=14..22,y=38..46 and color-11 pattern near x=14..22,y=15..19 are candidate terminal/status machinery.

## Competing hypotheses
- H1: leaving the level-2 glyph always teleports to a fixed checkpoint and refills the meter.
- H2: ACTION1 while on the glyph is a directional portal edge; other departure directions may act normally or lead elsewhere.
- H3: glyph activation changes maze phase/status, with the apparent teleport being the phase's designated spawn.
