# Working notes

## Grounded objects and controls
- 64x64 maze on 5x5 logical tiles; ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left, ACTION4=right.
- Player is a 5x5 token (two color-12 rows over three color-9 rows).
- Color 3 is route; 4/5 walls/background. Embedded 0/1 and 11 glyphs are mechanisms.
- Bottom color-11 strip is an action meter; level 2 spends two columns per ordinary input.

## Mechanisms established
- Level 1 floor switch toggles status; upward activation at upper dock completes level.
- Level 2 lower-right 0/1 switch initially creates a delayed hazard and consumes a life mark; later revisits alter warnings but charge ordinary meter cost.
- When the level-2 meter reaches zero, the next input causes a delayed respawn to checkpoint x=29,y=40, refills all 42 meter columns, restores the upper patterned 11 glyph at tile x=14,y=15, and changes bottom status marks. Full backtest passes 90/90.
- Entering the restored upper patterned tile at x=14,y=15 refills the meter. Leaving it makes the glyph disappear, so it is a collected key/recharge objective.
