# Working notes

## Grounded objects and controls
- 64x64 maze built on 5x5 logical tiles.
- Recorded controls: ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left, ACTION4=right.
- Player is a 5x5 token (two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows).
- Color 3 is traversable route; colors 4/5 mostly walls/background. Embedded 0/1 and 11 glyphs are interactive mechanisms.
- Bottom color-11 strip is an action meter; level 2 spends two columns per ordinary input.

## Certified mechanisms
- Level 1 floor switch toggles status; upward activation at its upper dock completes level 1.
- Level 2 lower-right 0/1 switch is hazardous and changes warning/life status.
- Meter exhaustion has two outcomes selected by the warning glyph: a right-hand warning mark causes GAME_OVER, while an earlier left-hand phase recovers at checkpoint x=29,y=40 and refills the meter.
- Entering the upper patterned tile at x=14,y=15 refills the meter to 42 columns. This was just reconfirmed after the post-GAME_OVER reset.
- Overall goal is WIN / 7 completed levels.
