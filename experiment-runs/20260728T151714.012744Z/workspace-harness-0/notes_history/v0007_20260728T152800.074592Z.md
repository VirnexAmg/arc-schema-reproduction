# Working notes

## Objects
- 64x64 tile maze with 5x5 logical cells.
- Player token is two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows.
- Color 3 is traversable floor; colors 4/5 are walls/background.
- Bottom color-11 strip is the ordinary-action budget meter.
- Embedded 0/1 glyphs are persistent floor switches and reappear after departure.
- Patterned chambers/status glyphs encode switch and terminal state.

## Established mechanisms
- ACTION1/2/3/4 are up/down/left/right by one 5-pixel tile.
- Traversable cells are solid color 3 or embedded 0/1 glyph cells; the upper terminal has a special dock.
- Entering the level-1 glyph toggles the status display and does not consume ordinary meter.
- Vacating that glyph restores its pattern.
- After activation, pressing ACTION1 at the upper dock completed level 1 and loaded level 2.
- Meter cost is level-dependent: level 1 ordinary actions consume one color-11 column; both observed level-2 ordinary actions consume two columns. The latest mismatch falsified the prior level-invariant one-column meter rule.

- Persistent 0/1 glyph at x=49..53,y=45..49 is likely the switch.
- Patterned color-11 tile at x=39..43,y=50..54 is likely the terminal or another mechanism.
