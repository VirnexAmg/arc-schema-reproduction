# Working notes

## Objects
- 64x64 scene with a 5x5 moving token. Its normal sprite is color 12 on its top two rows and color 9 on its bottom three rows.
- Static terrain uses colors 3/4/5. The token currently occupies x=34..38, y=20..24.
- Rows 61..62 contain a progress meter: each successful token move changes the leftmost color-11 column to color 3.
- A patterned target/terminal occupies the token lane around y=15..19.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- H1: ACTION1 moves the token upward by exactly 5 pixels; ACTION2 moves it downward by exactly 5 pixels.
- Successful vertical movement advances the bottom meter by one column. A blocked move changes neither token nor meter.
- Upward movement is blocked once the token is at y=15, explaining the seventh ACTION1 identity transition.
- ACTION3/ACTION4 are unknown; likely horizontal controls, but no transition evidence exists yet.
- Goal may require moving the token through a route rather than merely reaching the visible top terminal, since touching y=15 did not level up.

## Experiments and evidence
- Six consecutive ACTION1 transitions moved the token from approximately y=45 to y=15 in 5-pixel increments.
- A further ACTION1 at y=15 was a no-op, ruling out unrestricted upward motion.
- ACTION2 then moved it from y=15 to y=20 and advanced the meter, supporting ACTION2=down and meter advancement on successful movement.
