# Working notes

## Grounded objects and controls
- 64x64 maze built on 5x5 logical tiles.
- Recorded controls: ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left, ACTION4=right.
- Player is a 5x5 token (two color-12 rows above three color-9 rows).
- Color 3 is traversable route; colors 4/5 mostly walls/background. Embedded 0/1 and 11 glyphs are interactive mechanisms.
- Bottom color-11 strip is an action meter; in level 2 it spends two columns per ordinary input.

## Mechanisms established from transitions
- Level 1 floor switch toggles status; upward activation at the upper dock completes level 1.
- Level 2 lower-right 0/1 switch initially creates a delayed hazard and consumes a life/status mark; later revisits alter warnings but cost the ordinary meter amount.
- Exhausting the level-2 meter causes the following input to respawn the token at checkpoint x=29,y=40, refill 42 meter columns, restore the upper patterned 11 glyph at x=14,y=15, and alter bottom status marks.
- Entering the restored upper patterned tile refills the meter; leaving it reveals ordinary route, indicating a collectible/recharge objective.
