# Working notes

## Grounded objects and controls
- 64x64 maze on 5x5 logical tiles.
- Recorded controls: ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left, ACTION4=right.
- Player is a 5-wide color-12 cap over a color-9 body; color 3 is route floor.
- Bottom color-11 strip is an action meter; level 2 spends two columns per ordinary input.

## Current state
- Level 2, player at tile x=54,y=40.
- The latest ACTION2 moved the player down from y=35 to y=40 and consumed the final two meter columns. Thus the prior failed ACTION1 was caused by a blocked tile above y=35, not a hidden phase gate on the downward route.
- The ordinary meter is now empty. The status display has a split upper band, right-mark middle band, full lower band, and four reserve pixels.

## Mechanism hypotheses
- H1: The next input with the now-empty meter triggers checkpoint recovery at x=29,y=40 and refills the meter, independent of requested direction.
- H2: Empty-meter behavior is status-phase dependent. This four-reserve display is recoverable, unlike the fresh-life two-reserve/right-mark phase that produced GAME_OVER.

## Confirmed / ruled out
- ACTION1=up, ACTION2=down, ACTION3=left, ACTION4=right.
- A blocked input can consume charge when more than the protected final unit remains.
- The final two-column unit can be spent by a successful movement.
- Meter exhaustion does not resolve on the action consuming the last unit; resolution occurs on the following input.
- The lower-right 0/1 switch is reusable.
- Ruled out: the latest ACTION2 was not an exhaustion recovery; it was an ordinary successful move that reduced charge from two columns to zero.
