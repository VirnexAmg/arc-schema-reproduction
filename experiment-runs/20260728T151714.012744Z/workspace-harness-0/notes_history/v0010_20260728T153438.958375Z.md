# Working notes

## Objects and controls
- 64x64 maze organized mostly as 5x5 logical tiles.
- Player token: two color-12 rows over three color-9 rows.
- ACTION1/2/3/4 are up/down/left/right by one logical tile.
- Solid color 3 tiles are traversable; colors 4/5 are walls/background. Patterned 0/1 and 11 tiles can be special traversable machinery.
- Bottom color-11 strip is an ordinary-action meter: level 1 costs one column per ordinary action, level 2 costs two.

## Established mechanisms
- Level 1: entering the 0/1 floor switch changes status without meter cost; upward activation at the enabled upper dock completes the level.
- Level 2: entering the lower-right 0/1 glyph exhausts the meter and changes warning/status glyphs. The next action while standing there respawns the player at x=29,y=40, restores the trigger, refills the meter, and advances the life/status display.
- Level 2 patterned 11 chamber at x=14,y=15 is traversable. Entering it from x=14,y=10 refills the full meter and does not continue ordinary movement through the chamber; it is a recharge dock.

## Current state and route hypothesis
- Player is at the recharge dock x=14,y=15 with a full 42-column meter (21 level-2 actions).
- Candidate next terminal is patterned 11 tile x=39,y=50. A route to its eastern neighbor x=44,y=50 appears to require 17 actions: up to y=10, east to x=49, south to y=30, west to x=44, south to y=50.
- Entering x=39,y=50 is left unmodeled and should be tested separately after reaching its neighbor.

## Competing hypotheses
- H1: x=39,y=50 is the level completion terminal, likely activated by entering or pressing farther left while docked.
- H2: it is another recharge/checkpoint dock in a sequence.
- H3: completion requires a phase/status established by the lower-right trigger; patterned terminals share geometry but have different roles.
