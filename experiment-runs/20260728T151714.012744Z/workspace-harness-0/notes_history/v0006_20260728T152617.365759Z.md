# Working notes

## Objects
- 64x64 maze; player is a 5x5 token (two color-12 rows over three color-9 rows).
- Color 3 is traversable floor; 4/5 are mostly walls/background.
- Bottom color-11 strip is a move budget meter.
- Embedded 0/1 glyphs are persistent floor switches: they are covered while occupied and reappear after departure.
- The upper patterned chamber contains the level exit/terminal.

## Established mechanisms
- ACTION1/2/3/4 map to up/down/left/right by one 5-pixel tile.
- Successful ordinary moves and ordinary collisions advance the bottom meter.
- Glyph entry is traversable, changes the status display, and does not advance the ordinary meter.
- Vacating a glyph restores its 0/1 pattern; the earlier consumption hypothesis was ruled out by transition 21.
- With the switch inactive, ACTION1 at the upper dock is a no-op. After stepping on the glyph and returning, ACTION1 at that same dock completes the level and loads a new maze.

## Level progression
- Level 1 completed on the second upward press at the upper dock after activating the glyph.
- Level 2 starts with token at x=34..38,y=40..44 and has a persistent glyph at x=49..53,y=45..49 plus an apparent terminal/glyph at x=39..43,y=50..54.
