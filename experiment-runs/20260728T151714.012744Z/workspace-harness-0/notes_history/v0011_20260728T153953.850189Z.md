# Working notes

## Objects and controls
- 64x64 maze organized mostly as 5x5 logical tiles.
- Player token: two color-12 rows over three color-9 rows.
- ACTION1/2/3/4 are up/down/left/right by one logical tile.
- Solid color 3 tiles are traversable; colors 4/5 are walls/background. Patterned 0/1 and 11 tiles are special machinery.
- Bottom color-11 strip is an ordinary-action meter; level 2 spends two columns per ordinary action.

## Established mechanisms
- Level 1: entering the 0/1 floor switch changes status without meter cost; upward activation at the enabled upper dock completes the level.
- Level 2 upper patterned-11 chamber at x=14,y=15 is a traversable recharge dock.
- Lower-right 0/1 tile x=49,y=45 has a one-time dangerous phase tied to the bottom-right life mark. On first activation it exhausts the meter; the next input respawns at x=29,y=40, refills the meter, and removes a life mark.
- After that life mark is gone, revisiting the lower-right switch only toggles warning glyphs and costs the normal two meter columns. Moving off it restores its 0/1 floor glyph; it no longer forces respawn.

## What the mismatch falsified
- Falsified: every visit to x=49,y=45 exhausts the meter and every subsequent input there respawns.
- Falsified: the trigger is consumed after its first activation. It is restored whenever vacated.
- Evidence supports gating the hazardous behavior by the still-present bottom-right color-8 life mark.
