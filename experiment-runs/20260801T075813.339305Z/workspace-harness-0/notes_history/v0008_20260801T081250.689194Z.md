- The controllable object is the 5x5 purple-over-dark-red tile.
- Actions 1, 2, 3, and 4 are up, down, left, and right on a five-pixel lattice.
- The black/blue motif is a persistent floor switch rather than a collectible: it is hidden while the tile overlaps it and reappears when the tile leaves.

- The upper gray panel is traversable after synchronization; its lower edge is not itself the exit boundary.

- H_actor_motion (supported): cardinal movement preserves the tile pattern and restores the underlying terrain or object when the tile leaves.

- H_glyph_gate (supported, revised): the black/blue motif is a persistent switch whose overlap synchronizes the displayed glyph.
- H_panel_match (supported): switch activation makes the doubled lower display equal the small upper target.
- H_panel_entry (supported): after synchronization the actor can move through the panel's lower gray edge.
- H_exit_panel (active, revised): completion should occur when the actor advances fully into the panel's central 5x5 target area, not merely when it touches the lower edge.
