# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- The controllable object is the 5x5 purple-over-dark-red tile.
- The black/blue motif is stationary and occupies the lattice cell directly below the tile's current position.
- Color 3 is traversable/background terrain. Successful movement restores the tile's old footprint to green.
- The central yellow notch blocks the direct lower route, but the confirmed cardinal controls successfully carried the tile around its upper end.
- The narrow upper corridor leads to a gray panel containing a dark-red glyph. A larger related glyph panel is displayed at bottom-left.
- Every observed action consumes one cyan status-bar column.

## Hypotheses
- H_actor_motion (supported): actions 1, 2, 3, and 4 move the tile up, down, left, and right by one five-pixel lattice cell when the destination is ordinary green terrain.
- H_room_navigation (supported): movement over green terrain preserves the tile pattern and restores green beneath its previous footprint.
- H_action_timer (supported): every submitted action advances the bottom status bar by one column.
- H_action4_right (supported): action 4 is the rightward inverse of action 3.
- H_glyph_gate (uncertain): entering the black/blue motif may collect or transform it, enabling progress toward the upper glyph panel.
- H_motif_block (active alternative): the motif may instead behave as a solid non-green obstacle, so a down action would leave the tile in place while only advancing the timer.

## Evidence
- Evidence 1-4 established the four cardinal controls.
- Evidence 5-11: the route up four and left three executed exactly as predicted, confirming traversability and collision geometry around the yellow wall.
- The tile is now at rows 25-29 and columns 19-23, exactly one down action from overlapping the motif cell at rows 30-34.
