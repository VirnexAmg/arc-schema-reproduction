# Working notes

## Grounded objects
- The controllable object is the 5x5 purple-over-dark-red tile.
- Actions 1, 2, 3, and 4 are up, down, left, and right on a five-pixel lattice.
- The black/blue motif was consumable: moving onto it erased the motif and placed the tile in its cell.
- Motif collection did not advance the cyan timer.
- Collection transformed the large lower-left glyph from `111/100/101` to `111/001/101`, exactly matching the small upper target glyph.
- The narrow upper corridor leads directly to the gray target panel.

## Hypotheses
- H_actor_motion (supported): cardinal movement over green terrain preserves the tile pattern and restores green beneath it.
- H_action_timer (revised supported): ordinary actions consume one timer column, but successful motif collection does not.
- H_glyph_gate (supported): the black/blue motif is a collectible switch that synchronizes the large displayed glyph with the small upper target.
- H_motif_block (rejected): the tile successfully entered and consumed the motif cell.
- H_panel_match (supported): the motif applies an object-level glyph edit to make the lower display equal the upper target.
- H_exit_panel (active): after synchronization, contacting the upper gray target panel completes the level.

## Evidence
- Evidence 1-4 established the controls.
- Evidence 5-11 confirmed the route around the central wall.
- Evidence 12 falsified solid-obstacle behavior: the tile moved down, consumed the motif, changed the lower glyph to the target pattern, and did not consume timer.

## Candidate exit route
- From the collected motif cell, move up once to the broad passage, right three times to align with the vertical corridor, then up once to the cell immediately below the panel.
- A final up action contacts the synchronized upper panel and tests H_exit_panel.
